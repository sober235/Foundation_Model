## Critical

### C1. Volumes are not brought to one orientation; the sided labels are mirrored between sources in the model's frame

- Where: `anatobind/aur/dataset.py:27-35` (`load_volume` reads `np.asarray(img.dataobj)` as stored), `anatobind/aur/crops.py:16-21` (`to_zyx` only transposes), `anatobind/aur/crops.py:53-56` (`coordinates_mm` = index × spacing, no affine).
- Measured over every case's anatomy map (1570 files): PDGM **LPS** (501), ISLES **LAS** (250), SibBMS **RAS** (358), BMSR **RAS** (452) + **LAS** (9: 100106A, 100219A, 100246A, 100285B, 100296B, 100304A, 100357B, 100373A, 100414B). So along the model's x axis the patient's left lies at low x for PDGM and ISLES and at high x for SibBMS and 452 BMSR cases, and at low x again for 9 BMSR cases; the anterior direction is also opposite for PDGM.
- What is wrong: the entity queries are identity-anchored (query k = `ENTITY_LABELS[k]`, left white matter, right white matter, …) and the host classes are sided, with no mirroring allowed precisely so that the side is learnable. With mixed orientations the sides are anatomically correct inside each volume but reflected between sources in the array: this is the implicit mirroring the global constraint forbids (S4 lesson A17, where the same symptom killed the sided classes). Nothing in the input tells left from right (RoPE is translation-invariant, `local` is symmetric, brains are nearly symmetric), so the only way to fit the data is to recognise the source (skull-stripped 240 × 240 × 155 PDGM, MNI-registered SibBMS, skull-on BMSR, DWI) and condition the side on it. The §7 gates run on held-out patients of the same sources and would not see this; the 9 LAS BMSR cases are side-label noise against their 452 RAS peers; the external checks (fastMRI RSS frame, the SibBMS annotation subset which is stored LAS) and any `--nifti` input of Part 2 get a side guess. The relation geometry's side feature was made frame-robust in fix round 1 (`relation.py:63-69`), but the entity and host identities were not.
- Evidence the repo knows how: S4 canonicalised (`scripts/brain_anatomy_prepare.py:47`, `nib.as_closest_canonical`; STATUS of S4: "左右跟文件头走"); the knee line has `anatobind/infer/canonical.py`. Part 1 dropped it. For all four sources `as_closest_canonical` is a pure flip / permutation (checked: shapes and zooms carried over, no resampling), so it does not conflict with N4.
- Fix (now, before the tag; small): in `load_volume`, reorient the image, the anatomy and the lesion map with `nib.as_closest_canonical` (RAS) before `to_zyx`, and take the spacing from the reoriented header; add to `tests/test_aur_dataset.py` a case that saves an LPS copy of the test volume (flip axes 0 and 1 of the array and of the affine) and asserts `load_volume` gives the same `image`, `entity`, `instance`, `spacing` and `hosts` as the RAS original; state in `SwinBackbone` / `make_crop` docstrings and the README that arrays are RAS before zyx. `samples.json` and `grids.txt` are unaffected (the check compares headers). Part 2: inference applies the same reorientation and writes `anatomy.nii.gz` / `lesions.nii.gz` back in the input's orientation (`ornt_transform` / `inv_ornt_aff` as in `canonical.py`).

## Important

### I1. The spec says the data is 1 mm (ISLES 2 mm); BMSR and ISLES are not, the crop's physical extent varies three- to five-fold, and none of this is in a record

- Where: spec §0 ("四个来源的 1 mm 数据"), §3.1 (BMSR "同网格" only; ISLES "2 mm"), §6 ("1 mm 下 128 × 160 × 160 体素的裁块"); README "Known deviations" (silent); `p0/` (no spacing table); `anatobind/aur/crops.py:12` (`CROP` in voxels), `crops.py:101` (rotation in voxel space).
- Measured (anatomy headers, one per case): PDGM 1 mm (501), SibBMS 1 mm (358); ISLES 2 × 2 × 2 (196) but 54 cases with 4.4–5.0 mm slices (shapes 128 × 128 × 25, 192 × 192 × 30, 1.15–1.8 mm in-plane); BMSR in-plane 0.43–1.17 mm and slices 1.0–5.0 mm (186 at 0.859 × 0.859 × 1.5, 85 at 1.172 × 1.172 × 1.5, 56 at 0.688 × 0.688 × 1.5, 46 at 0.75 × 0.75 × 2.0, 31 at ≤ 0.5 mm in-plane, 9 with 3–5 mm slices), and 12 BMSR cases whose thick axis is y, not z (100108C, 100115A, 100154A, 100192A, 100203A, 100238B, 100253A, 100300A, 100300D, 100301A, 100305C, 100312A; e.g. 0.5 × 1.0 × 0.5).
- Why it matters: with a fixed 128 × 160 × 160-voxel crop the physical field of view is 69–188 mm in-plane and 128–640 mm along z for BMSR (at 0.43 mm a 69 mm crop holds a quarter of the head and often one hemisphere, so the side feature of `relation.geometry` falls back to 0; at 5 mm slices 80 % of the crop is padding), 183–320 mm / 256–614 mm for ISLES; the token pitch against RoPE's 6.3–1117 mm wavelengths and the volume floor in voxels (10 mm³ = 55 voxels at 0.43 × 0.43 × 1.0) vary twenty-fold; the ±10° rotation in voxel space is an anisotropic (sheared) rotation for the 12 y-thick cases. The design (variable size, physical RoPE) can ingest all of it, but whether to train on voxel crops of such different extents, to harmonise BMSR / ISLES offline (a derived dataset under `derived/aur/`; N4 forbids resizing inside the model, not harmonising a source), or to use per-source crop sizes is a decision the user was never shown, and §7's "1 mm 测试卷" and the paper would carry a false statement.
- Fix (now, records only; the decision goes to the user): write the per-source orientation / spacing / shape table into `p0/spacing.txt` (the one-off script output, with the command), add a README "Known deviations" line and a dated note in spec §3.1, and put the choice (voxel crops as is / offline harmonisation of BMSR and ISLES / per-source crops) into `STATUS.md` §2 before the Part 2 plan is written.

### I2. DDP fails on the second step of any Stage II style step or any batch whose U is unsupervised everywhere (Part 2 trainer; the measured schedule did not include the remedy)

- Where: the interface `AnatoBindBrain.forward` + `losses.event_loss` / `bind` as used by `scripts/aur_probe.py:41-66`; the probe always computed A + S + U + R with every sample supervised, so it never saw it.
- Reproduced on CPU (gloo, world 1, tiny model): a step without `bind` (Stage II) or with `u_supervised = [False, False]` leaves `relation.*` (and the event decoder's parameters) without gradients; `DistributedDataParallel` raises "Expected to have finished reduction in the prior iteration … parameters that were not used in producing loss" on the second step. With real batches this happens on the first all-SibBMS / all-T1 batch of a rank.
- Why it matters: the Part 2 trainer must either pass `find_unused_parameters=True` (a graph walk every step; the 0.90 s / step in spec §6 was measured without it), or freeze `model.relation` in Stage II (`requires_grad_(False)`) and add a zero-weighted touch of the event presence / mask logits when no sample of a rank's batch supervises U (or draw batches so that every rank always holds a U-supervised crop). The choice belongs to the Part 2 plan, and the step time should be re-measured with it.
- Fix: no Part 1 code change; record it in the README's Part 2 notes and in `STATUS.md` §3 so the Part 2 plan starts from it.

## Part 2 notes (what will bite the trainer, the evaluator and the inference chain)

1. **Orientation (C1)**: after the fix every training array is RAS before zyx; inference must canonicalise the input the same way and write `anatomy.nii.gz` / `lesions.nii.gz` back in the input's own orientation (`ornt_transform`, `inv_ornt_aff`); B0 (geometric lookup on the same SynthSeg map) is frame-independent, so the comparison stays fair. Keep the sided-class sanity check from S4 A17 (left and right pseudo-Dice rising together in the first epochs).
2. **Spacing (I1)**: the evaluator's "1 mm 测试卷" is a native-grid evaluation for BMSR / ISLES; Dice against SynthSeg is on the same grid either way; the per-lesion matching uses `MIN_MM3` in mm³ (fine). The user's decision on harmonisation must precede the Part 2 plan, since it changes `samples.json` (new derived files) or the dataset.
3. **DDP (I2)**: `find_unused_parameters=True`, or freeze `model.relation` in Stage II and touch the event outputs with weight 0 when a rank's batch has no U-supervised crop; re-measure the step with the chosen remedy. `use_checkpoint=False`; `NCCL_P2P_DISABLE=1`; `timeout` on every multi-card command.
4. **Targets the dataset does not emit**: `present (B, K)` for `entity_loss` (M6); the validation split (10 % of the training patients, spec §6) is not in `samples.json` — carve it by patient with a fixed seed and record it; `sample_points(valid, N_POINTS, generator, instance=batch["instance"])` must be called with the instance map (the ledger's ⚠️); `event_targets_at_points(gather(batch["instance"], pts), batch["n_instances"])` gives the Hungarian targets; `relation_loss(model.bind(out, b, qi), batch["host"][b][ti], batch["negatives"][b][ti])` as in the probe; `matches[b] is None` for unsupervised U → skip `bind`; `bind` with an empty `qi` returns `(0, 14)` and the loss is zero (verified).
5. **Inference**: sliding windows must contain a valid voxel (the query decoder divides by zero otherwise); the §4.1 rule "argmax among present entities" needs a presence threshold (0.5 on `sigmoid(entity_presence)`; `bind` already gates by the soft presence); `pix["full"]` is dense per window (32 × 128 × 160 × 160 bf16 ≈ 210 MB per sample) — fine on an A800, mind it on CPU.
6. **Budget and loader**: ISLES (and 4.8 mm BMSR) volumes are smaller than the crop in every axis, so the two crops per epoch are the same window (M7); `load_volume` reads and relabels the whole volume per item (two crops) and `host_targets` is O(instances × volume) (M11) — with 8 workers per rank the 0.90 s step should hide it, but measure the loader alone first.
7. **Open pre-flight items**: P5 (matching rule and the S7 out-of-fold prediction paths per case) and the rest of P4 (the 10 SibBMS annotated subjects: their FLAIR / mask grid, stored LAS) are not done (M8) — both belong before the evaluator is written.
8. **Loss shaping to watch**: with one lesion in a crop, 8192 of the 16 384 points are lesion voxels that carry no entity loss (entity supervision effectively on the uniform half); the hinge's duplicate negative (T3); the matching cost ignoring `point_weight` (M12).
